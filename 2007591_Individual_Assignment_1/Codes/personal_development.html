Reflection on The Richest Man in Babylon

"Seven Cures for a Lean Purse" (Clason, 1926)

The chapter teaches seven main ways to improve your financial situation. These include saving at least a portion of your income, controlling expenses, growing your savings, protecting your money, planning for the future, and improving your ability to earn. I can apply these lessons to my life by building good financial habits early on. As a student, I may not have a large income, but I can still learn to save some of what I receive and avoid unnecessary spending. The lesson about increasing my ability to earn matters to me because I am studying Computing and want to become a software engineer. Improving my programming and problem-solving skills can increase my future opportunities

"Five Laws of Gold" (Clason, 1926)

The Five Laws of Gold explain that money grows when a person consistently saves, invests carefully, protects their money, and seeks advice from knowledgeable people. The chapter also warns against investing in things you don't understand or chasing promises of quick wealth. I can apply these lessons by being careful with my financial decisions and learning about money before making major choices. Instead of trying to become wealthy quickly, I want to focus on building my career, saving consistently, and making responsible financial decisions.
